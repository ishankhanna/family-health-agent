# Future product spec

What this could become: a **family health companion** you keep using, not a chat you open only when someone is already sick.

A family should not retell its story every time someone feels off. One place for Aarav’s fever, Priya’s migraine, and Meera’s knees — often the same parent, in the same hour. Today’s rash is still that rash tonight. Meera’s knees build up over months.

**Where it is used.** On the caregiver’s phone, mostly WhatsApp. Later: voice, and Hindi / other Indian languages, so grandparents can use it too. India first (108, Crocin, Dolo, joint families), then any home that shares care. A family clinic or hospital can sit behind the same chat: the family keeps history between visits; the doctor gets a one-page “what changed,” not a blank form.

**Why this, not ChatGPT.** A general chat does not know your family. You explain who “he” is again, paste last night’s temperature, and hope it remembers. It may soften chest pain. This product *is* the family file. It remembers people and open problems. A follow-up sticks without being asked. If it is unsure who you mean, it asks once instead of guessing. Emergency and doctor messages are fixed rules. The model talks; the product decides when talking is no longer enough.

**North star.** Simple home care stays in the chat. An optional “how is he today?” ping updates the problem — no reply does not mean he is fine. For small issues that still need a doctor, the case is already in a partner clinic queue, with a short summary (symptoms, timeline, what we still don’t know). The family can see requested / accepted / done. No extra Practo booking. No retelling at the desk. Real emergencies skip the queue and go to 108. If the hospital link is down, the product says so. It never pretends a transfer happened.

Labs, wearables, and a knowledge base can come later. Remembering the family and a real doctor link come first.

**What it will not do.** It does not prescribe on its own. Adults own their records. Living in the same house is not a free pass to Meera’s file. People can look at, fix, export, or delete their data. Doctors stay in the loop on when to escalate.

Not a diagnosis machine. A front door to care — with two doors the model is not allowed to make up.
